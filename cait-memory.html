Memory
The page-state switcher is out of the panel for now, so an early reviewer meets the populated page rather than a row of states to audit. The states themselves are untouched — empty, one module empty, loading, partial, unavailable and failed all still render. Reach them from the console: S.mem.page='partial'; renderCanvas().
Conflict UX
Inline puts the conflict on the record it is about — provenance is exact, and four of them is four tiles spread over five screens. Guided is one banner at the top, one question at a time, with a queue that counts down; the record keeps a badge so it still says it is unsettled. Skip moves the queue on without deciding. Desktop only — a fixed banner plus a full record list is a laptop layout, and on a phone the banner would be most of the screen.
